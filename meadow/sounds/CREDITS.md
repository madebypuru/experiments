# Sheep sounds

All recordings are from Wikimedia Commons and are public domain or CC0, so no attribution is required. They're credited here anyway.

| Recording | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sheep bleating.ogg | Public domain (clipped from the PDSounds recording "Corner of a sheep field in summer") | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sheep_bleating.ogg |
| Corner of a sheep field in summer.ogg | Public domain (earthcalling, via PDSounds) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Corner_of_a_sheep_field_in_summer.ogg |
| Sheep bleat.ogg | CC0 (Maan Farms, Abbotsford, BC) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sheep_bleat.ogg |
| Sheep in Antefasy.wav | CC0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sheep_in_Antefasy.wav |

`sounds.js` holds eight single bleats cut from these, plus the field recording as a quiet background. The bleats are high-passed and level-matched, then encoded as AAC. They're inlined as base64 so the page also works when it's opened straight from disk. The originals and the cut clips are in `../../_source/meadow-sounds/`.

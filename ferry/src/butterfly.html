Ferry · icon + wordmark

Light, effortless passage

The mark is a butterfly drawn in steps: two wings facing across a narrow body, the notch on each outer edge splitting fore- and hindwing. Butterflies cross whole continents on almost no effort; Ferry carries value across chains the same way. The wordmark is Ferry Sans Bold, the brand's own typeface, set to the devx construction. Ferry Sans softens every outer corner with half the butterfly's corner radius, so letters and wings feel cut from one material.

Construction

Measured from the devx lockup in Figma, with A = the height of the d (ascender to baseline). The mark is 1.63A tall, sits 0.35A above the ascender and 0.28A below the baseline, and stands 0.74A from the first letter.

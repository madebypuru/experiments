Bleed, a tad

Ferry Sans Bold, hand-spaced to the devx rhythm, then laid down like wet pigment: edges wander a little, colour pools darker at the rim, the body has paper grain, and where letters sit close (the double r, the r into the y) they run into each other. The butterfly gets the same treatment. On the wash, the white reads as paper left bare, with the wash pooling against its edge.

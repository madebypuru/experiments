# Ferry palette

Eight colours, sampled from the gouache studies and painted by the Paint engine. `system.html` in this folder shows them. Grid, formats and use cases come later, one touchpoint at a time (drafts in `LATER.md`).

| Name | Hex | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F2EEE6` | The light field. Mats around paintings, the mark on dark. |
| Ink | `#1B262B` | The one text colour on light. |
| Harbour | `#2C454B` | The dark field, deep water. The mark's home. |
| Sea glass | `#6B8A94` | Mid water and sky. |
| Mist | `#C9CDBD` | Pale sky. Tinted fields. |
| Stone | `#D2A57F` | Warm stone and timber. |
| Sea grass | `#7D8F69` | Quiet green. |
| Signal | `#D2602C` | Life-ring orange. |

## Three rules

1. **Paintings can be any colour. Everything around them uses only these eight.**
2. **Text is Ink on the light colours, or Paper on Harbour and Ink.** Sea glass, Sea grass and Signal are too weak for reading text.
3. **Signal appears once.** One button, one dot, one life ring.

The painted swatches in `swatches/` are cut from `../scenes/palette.svg` painted in gouache. Paint moves each colour a little, as real paint does; the hex values above are the reference.

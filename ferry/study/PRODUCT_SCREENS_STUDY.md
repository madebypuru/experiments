# Product screens study

Method: Mobbin through its MCP (web screens, 14 searches, images looked at), plus the live send forms of bridges in a browser pane without connecting a wallet. Sizes are by eye. Anything not seen is marked (inferred). Chrome extension was not connected, so the live look used the in-app Browser pane at 0.6 scale. Links are Mobbin screen URLs (prefix https://mobbin.com/screens/) or site URLs.

## Send

1. Uniswap swap (https://mobbin.com/screens/07e30421-8467-4982-879d-f0a8a1f03b67). A card about 240 wide in a 768 frame (about 31 percent of the width), centred, with a wide empty page around it. Tabs Swap, Limit, Send, Buy sit top left of the card in small grey type, a cog top right. Two stacked panels "Sell" and "Buy" in pale grey, the amount "0" at about 20 px in light grey against 7 px labels, the token chip at right (ETH with a round logo and chevron). Between them a small round arrow button overlaps both panels. The primary button is a full-width grey pill reading "Select a token" until a pair exists, then the brand pink. Fee and time are not on the form at all. Looks calm but the chrome (nav, search, address pill) is busy; the pink token button is the single brand moment.
2. OKX Convert (https://mobbin.com/screens/eb701f32-c84c-47b6-bb63-bf098400d992). Card on the right half of a marketing page, headline "Convert ETH to USDC" at left in about 28 px black. Card: title "Convert" with a lime "0 Fees" tag, From panel with Available balance and a Max pill, amount right aligned at about 12 px bold, dollar value under it in grey. "To" panel mirrors it. One line of rate in grey, then a black full-width pill "Preview". The form is a step before review, so the button says Preview, not Convert. The lime tag looks cheap beside the black.
3. Binance Convert modal (https://mobbin.com/screens/13969d30-ba47-4449-a1aa-a8d35d427ba6). Dark modal about 200 wide over an app shell. Wallet checkboxes (Spot, Funding), From and To panels with a balance label top right of each, token chip at right, a Rate row with an info icon and a tooltip that explains the quote may differ from spot, then a yellow "Convert" button. Dense, heavy chrome, tooltip sits over the To amount. Dated and anxious.
4. Base Trade (https://mobbin.com/screens/09a5bbe0-2943-4077-9f53-eddf516df513). Modal about 230 wide. "You Pay" and "You Receive" panels; amount at about 22 px in black, 3 times the label size, which is the strongest amount hierarchy of the set. Under the pay amount: dollar value with a flip icon, balance and a blue "Use max" link. One saturated blue pill "Trade". No fee line on the form.
5. OpenSea swap drawer (https://mobbin.com/screens/6a69de36-4f28-4841-8294-f7a195fa87a1). Dark side drawer about 215 wide. Sell and Buy blocks with the amounts at about 12 px in mono-like type, a rate line "1.00 ETH = 334.83 UNI" with a gas estimate "$1.45" and chevron to expand, a blue "Swap" button. The rate and fee collapse into one line, which is a good compression. Dark with a blue button reads as the usual crypto look.
6. Family Send (https://mobbin.com/screens/c15daa40-a4a8-47fe-b3f9-70b64a6835d1). Page-width layout but the form is a 240 wide column centred in white space. Header "Send" centred with a back arrow, a thin teal progress line under the header that grows with each step (about 20, then 80, then 100 percent). "To" row with the address as a grey pill, then an Amount panel: label "Amount", "0.00" in light grey at about 14 px, token chip "ETH" at right, dollar value and "Balance: 0.021 Use Max" in 6 px grey. No button until valid. Quietest form of the set; the progress line replaces a stepper.
7. Wise amount (https://mobbin.com/screens/1491a8f2-b992-4859-81e4-c1939124e51b). Top bar with logo left, a four-label stepper centred (Recipient, Amount, Review, Pay) with a thin line above showing progress, avatar and close at right. Body column about 270 wide. "You send" with currency chip (flag, SGD, chevron) at left and "0.00" at right in heavy grey at about 20 px; second row "Tan gets" with USD. A rate pill "1 SGD = 0.7855 USD" above. The Continue button is a full-width grey pill that sits inside a soft box saying "Enter either amount to continue". After an amount (https://mobbin.com/screens/90b0251f-95ef-46e1-8562-e1975856f4dd shows the currency picker with a search field, a "Your recent currencies" group and "All currencies", rows as code plus full name, e.g. "USD United States dollar"). Codes with names spelled out is the same rule Ferry wants.
8. Wise amount, filled (inside the Wise send flow https://mobbin.com/flows/77c0a951-fd82-4d40-8146-e54549e9319b). "Tan gets exactly 4.99" with the amount in green at about 24 px, then four rows with a circle icon each: You send, Paying with, Arrives "Today - in seconds", Total fees "Free - no fees to pay", each with a small green "Change" or "Schedule" pill at right. An optional reference field. A green full-width "Continue". Time and fee are two rows in plain words, before any commitment. The best model of fee and time before sending in the set.
9. Airwallex new transfer (https://mobbin.com/screens/97f7a52d-edf4-4a73-a706-89108e6f4692). White card about 345 wide with an orange progress bar across the very top of the page. 10. Binance Send, step 2 (https://mobbin.com/screens/9c73308f-29b6-4f12-907c-43725d544b0a). Three-step numbered stepper in yellow discs joined by a line (Send to, Enter Amount, Confirm Payment). Form column about 195 wide: Send to, Currency, Amount with a USD toggle, optional Note with a 10/50 counter, "Previous Step" and "Continue" side by side. The optional note is a real pattern for the postcard message.
11. PayPal Send (https://mobbin.com/screens/baa37997-862a-47c1-a357-2eccd10d2f49). Card about 280 wide: recipient email, two amounts side by side "You send $0.00 USD" and "Recipient gets $0.00 SGD" at about 18 px, "Please enter amount to see exchange rate", then a "What's this for?" box, a pale disabled "Next" pill and a text link "Cancel". Looks dated: navy header band, 1990s card on white.
12. Mercury amount (https://mobbin.com/screens/0109778d-691a-4bf9-8836-16fd4649aef5). Left vertical stepper (Recipient, Amount, Details, Review) in small text with an active blue bar, content column about 285 wide: Recipient gets, Send from (account with balance), Send on "Today" with the line "Payment expected to arrive in 0-1 business days", a Repeat toggle. Back and Next pills at the foot. Time is stated as a sentence under the date.
13. Acctual new internal transfer (https://mobbin.com/screens/25867c08-95b3-4041-adbb-474e5804b379). A right side sheet about 275 wide: Memo, Amount with currency, then "Transfer details" with Asset, Network, Pay from, Pay to as four stacked selects; the Pay to dropdown lists saved wallets with a name, chain and shortened address. A dark "Ready for payment" button. Dense business form, field after field.
14. Stripe create a payment (https://mobbin.com/screens/3daf605d-36c3-4793-9269-135678e5dd62). 15. Whop buy tokens (https://mobbin.com/screens/b5f3bb6c-2fc1-497f-8386-29d3a6f7e763). Small modal with two panels side by side joined by a chevron: "Enter an amount" with preset chips $10, $25, $50, and "You will receive" with an orange bolt and "125,000" at about 16 px. Preset chips plus a result panel is a quick form for known amounts.
16. Across Protocol, live (https://app.across.to). Dark page (near black), centred headline "Bridge & swap across chains" in a thin light grey serif-like display at about 28 px, card about 300 wide below it. Before a wallet: "From" panel with "0.00" at about 24 px in grey and a disabled chain-and-token chip at right (greyed), "$0.00" under it with a flip icon; a small round arrow; "To" panel with "Set Recipient" and a pencil; then a green panel labelled "Across V5" holding a full-width mint button "CONNECT WALLET". Nav top: Transfer, History, Support, Docs, and a mint "CONNECT" pill. Fee and time are absent until a wallet and amount exist. History is a top-level tab. The mint on near-black is the neon crypto look Ferry rejects.
17. Relay bridge, live (https://relay.link/bridge). Light lavender page with a white rounded frame, card about 190 wide centred high on the page. Nav: Swap, Vaults, Transactions, a search "Search anything" with a slash key hint, a lavender "Connect" pill. Card: small cog top right; "Sell" panel with "0" at about 20 px bold black, chip "ETH Base" (token and chain name stacked in one chip, which is a good way to show chain with the asset), "Select wallet" link; arrow; "Buy" panel with "0" in grey and a bold italic "SELECT TOKEN" button; the primary button full width in deep purple, bold italic caps "CONNECT WALLET". Italic caps buttons feel loud. Transactions is a top-level tab.
18. Jumper, live (https://jumper.xyz). The first screen is a marketing hero ("Find the best route", stats 64 chains, 41 bridges, 47 DEXs) with the widget as a dimmed ghost behind the headline, tabs Swap & Bridge, Private, Gas, a cog, and a purple "Connect" pill. The form itself was not reachable by direct URL in the pane (it showed the hero again). Pitch-first, tool second. Dark with purple glow.
19. deBridge, live (https://app.debridge.com). Near-black page, a dimmed card behind a cookie bar. Visible, faintly: tabs, "You pay" with an ETH chip and "0", "You receive" with an ETH chip, a route line "Routing" with a loading state, and a yellow "Connect wallet" in the top bar. The page defaults to chain 1 to chain 10 through URL parameters (inputChain, outputChain), which is how a route can be a link. Yellow on dark navy. Could not read it clearly: the card stayed dim behind the cookie notice.
20. Uniswap, live. Attempted app.uniswap.org; it landed on an unrelated site in the pane, so no live reading. The Mobbin screen (item 1) stands in.

Live note: of the four bridge forms reached (Across, Relay, Jumper, deBridge), none shows fee or time before a wallet is connected. All four put the connect action as the primary button in place of the send action. None shows a stepper; the whole send is one card.

## Problems

53. OpenSea transaction error (https://mobbin.com/screens/e727a48c-a9b2-4cb0-bb37-9e132df9de31). ## Patterns that repeat

1. The send form is one card, 190 to 345 wide on a full page, centred, with a lot of empty page around it. Only Wise, Family and Airwallex add a progress line (top bar or stepper) for a short flow.
2. Two stacked panels with a small round arrow between them (Uniswap, OKX, Base, OpenSea, Across, Relay). Amount at left or right of its panel, chip at the other side. Ferry's FROM and TO should keep this stacked shape.
3. Amount hierarchy: the best forms set the amount 3 times the label size (Base about 22 against 7; Wise 20 against 6; Relay 20). The weakest keep it at 12 px beside a 10 px label (OKX, Binance).
4. Fee and time before sending are in plain words in a row or two, never a chart: "Arrives Today - in seconds / Total fees Free" (Wise), "Fee Estimate up to $0.03, Estimated Time ~30s" (Family), "Payment expected to arrive in 0-1 business days" (Mercury). The four live bridges show neither before connect.
5. The primary button is one full-width pill, and its label changes with the state: Select a token, Preview, Connect wallet, Continue, Send now. The best put the real action and amount in it (OpenSea "Send 0.0000001 ETH").
6. Review repeats the amount big, then label left, value right, hairline separators, with an edit link per row (Wise, Melio, Airwallex, Kraken). It is the postcard's facts table already.
7. In progress is almost always a spinner and one line ("Processing...") with a "do not close" warning, and no time, stage or progress. Only Wise (timestamped updates), Selfridges (a step line and a timeframe) and Acctual (a bar) show anything more.
8. Success is a check in a circle, one sentence with the amount, and two buttons (the nouns are Done, View receipt, Send again, New payment). The strongest add the exact timestamp to the second (Binance, OKX).
9. History rows: icon at left, name bold, a grey sub line "date . status", amount right aligned with the converted amount under it in grey, grouped under date headings, pending group on top (PayPal, Wise, Gemini). Status is a word in the sub line unless an operator needs filters; then a chip with a glyph (Stripe).
10. Detail is a label/value table, labels grey, values bold or black, right aligned, with copy icons on IDs (Kraken). An updates list with minute timestamps carries the story (Wise).
11. Errors name what failed in the headline, give one sentence and one button ("Try again"), and keep the form state. The calmest are grey (Uniswap) or a small red glyph in a grey banner (Wise); the loudest are solid orange or red bands and mascot illustrations.
12. Brand character is held to one object: Wise's chrome check on its end screen and lime buttons, Uniswap's pink token button, OKX's orbit rings around the check. Dated or cheap: stock illustrations, heavy blue headers, lime "0 Fees" tags, neon on black, bold italic caps buttons, exclamation marks.

## A screen-by-screen outline for Ferry

Fixed rules: Ferry Sans only; Paper #F2EEE6 ground, Ink #1B262B type, Harbour #2C454B for the primary button and ink rules, Sea glass #6B8A94 for labels and dotted route, Mist #C9CDBD for hairlines and the pier state, Stone #D2A57F and Sea grass #7D8F69 as small marks, Signal #D2602C once per view; no gradients; calm motion; chain codes with names spelled out where there is room; no exclamation marks. Width: one column 480 wide on desktop, 100 percent minus 16 px gutters on a phone, vertical rhythm in multiples of 4. Labels in spaced caps at 11 px (the ticket's 10 at scale), body 15 px, amounts 44 px, codes 30 px. The ticket is the same paper object everywhere, at its 84 size in lists and its 120 size as the hero, never recoloured.

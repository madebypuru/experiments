# Ferry: Brand Brief

Nov 11, 2026 · Puru Sharma

## Overview

Ferry is a cross-chain payments and bridging protocol that moves value between chains the way a ferry moves people: steady, on schedule, nothing lost on the way.

Ferry is a 37degree brand exercise. The name and logo are set by hand. Every other visual (paintings, motion, landing page, social) is generated through the paint engine and AI, from a reference for each asset. The finished brand doubles as a pitch to AI, SaaS and Web3 founders: proof that a technical product can feel human and calm.

Brand line (working): Safe passage between chains.

## Positioning

Ferry is the slow, safe boat in a category full of speedboats.

**The problem:** moving value between chains still feels risky. Bridges get hacked, transfers stall, fees surprise people, and most brands in the space answer with more neon and more speed claims.

**The audience:**
- Primary: Web3 founders and protocol teams who need to move value between chains for their users.
- Secondary: everyday users who have been burned by a bridge and want one they can trust.

**What we promise:** every transfer arrives, on time, intact.

**Why believe it:** visible status at every step, plain-language confirmations, and a brand that behaves the way it asks users to trust it: calm and predictable.

**Category frame:** infrastructure you don't think about, like a scheduled ferry service.

## Personality and voice

Ferry sounds like a good ferry captain: calm, on time, and clear about what happens next.

Three words: calm, dependable, crossing.

| Ferry is | Ferry is not |
|---|---|
| Calm | Hyped |
| Plain-spoken | Jargon-heavy |
| Warm, handmade | Neon, 3D chrome |
| Patient, on schedule | "Fastest bridge ever" |
| Confident | Degen, meme-y |

Voice rules:
- Short sentences. Say what happens, then stop.
- Use journey words naturally: departs, arrives, crossing, harbour. Never force the metaphor into every line.
- No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no emoji in brand copy.
- Numbers over adjectives: "arrives in 40 seconds", not "lightning fast".

Sample lines: Safe passage between chains. Your transfer has departed. Arrived. Every time.

## Visual foundations

Every Ferry visual is painted, not rendered: the paint engine is the brand's image system.

- **Logo (locked):** two blocky facing halves, two shores meeting, with a lowercase "ferry" wordmark. Shown white on a watercolour landscape, where the painted edge becomes the signature.
- **Medium:** watercolour as the main medium. Test gouache as a possible accent.
- **Image world:** water and crossings. Harbours, rivers and lakes, ferries under way, figures on bridges and piers. Soft light, wide horizons, lots of sky.
- **Motion:** built from the engine's bases: boats under way, water ripples, clouds, birds, walkers on bridges. Calm wind by default. Weather can carry meaning, for example clear sky for normal states and rain for incidents or launches. Loops sized for web hero and for X.
- **Colour:** sampled from the paintings, not picked up front. Expect sky and water blues, field greens and a warm ochre. White for the mark. One ink colour for text.
- **Type:** serif for titles and display only; sans for body and UI. The lowercase wordmark sets the tone: friendly, not shouty.
- **Layout:** bento grids and editorial layouts from the Pinterest references, with paintings bleeding to the edges and the mark cropped large as a supergraphic.

## Process

One brand touchpoint at a time, each built for its real use and signed off before the next starts. Puru supplies a reference for every asset.

Touchpoints still to make: medium lock, painted hero scenes (water and crossings), motion language, core system (colour from the paintings, type, grid, bento rules), landing page, social kit (X post templates, motion loops, OG image, profile and header), brand sheet.

Done: brief and logo.

The wordmark · Ferry Sans Bold

Set to the devx construction and hand-spaced: 35, 33, 6 and 1 units between f, e, r, r and y. The logo and the typeface are one family, and Ferry owns both outright.

01

Body and interface

Regular at 14 to 18 px, tracking 0, line height 1.5. Bold for values the reader is looking for, never for emphasis. Emphasis switches to italic.

02

Labels

Bold caps at 10 to 12 px with +14% to +16% tracking, in the muted colour. The functional layer, like the print on a ticket.

03

Large

Bold at −5% tracking, the wordmark's setting. Display headlines stay serif; large sans is for numbers and the wordmark. The watercolour bleed is for display sizes only, never text.
